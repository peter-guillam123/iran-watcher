What this tells us

The headline finding for 21 April: we landed the State Department's Mahan Air sanctions designation on the same day, which is exactly the kind of primary-source confirmation that ISW's procurement-networks theme rests on. We also caught the ceasefire-stalemate diplomatic story across four Iran International pieces.

The gap is sharp on (a) the tactical military layer — Hezbollah, IDF, Saudi militia drone strikes, Iraqi militia attacks — and (b) commercial maritime intelligence (Tanker Trackers, MarineTraffic) which ISW relies on for the blockade-effectiveness data. Both gaps are scope-known.

The strongest UK-newsroom angle on this day, that ISW doesn't touch at all, is the Iran-domestic-repression coverage: the Mashhad eyewitness reporting, the cemetery defacement, the pricing-out-of-bread household stories. These are independently publishable leads that wouldn't surface from an ISW-only diet.
